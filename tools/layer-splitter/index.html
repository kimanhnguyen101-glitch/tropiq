Layer Splitter

Cut a flat PNG or JPG into separate layers. Everything runs in your browser; images never leave your computer.

Drop a PNG or JPG here, or press Open image.

Click-to-cut: click an object to select it, click more spots to grow the selection, Shift-click (or right-click) to remove an area. Then press “Cut selection to new layer”. The cut comes from the layer highlighted on the right.
